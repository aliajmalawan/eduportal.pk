No surprises

What is included, and what costs extra

Most school ERP vendors in Pakistan will not publish this. Here is the whole picture before you talk to anyone.

Included in your monthly price

  • Every module marked for your plan in the comparison table above — no per-module licence fees
  • Your full student roll up to the plan limit — 300 on Silver, 800 on Gold, 1,200 on Diamond
  • The parent and teacher mobile apps on the plans that list them
  • Software updates released while your subscription is active

Billed separately

SMS credits
Text messages are charged against your own SMS gateway account. You pay your provider for the volume you send, so a school that sends few messages is not subsidising one that sends many.
WhatsApp messaging
Sent through the WhatsApp Business API, which Meta charges per conversation on your own account. EduPortal does not add a markup on top.
Setup & data import
A one-time cost, quoted per school. It depends on how many student, staff and fee records you are bringing across and what condition that data is in.
On-site staff training
Also quoted per school, based on how many campuses and staff need covering. Remote handover is part of normal onboarding.

These four are the only costs outside your plan price. Ask us for current rates and we will give you real figures, not a sales call.

FAQ

Pricing — frequently asked questions

EduPortal has three plans, billed monthly: Silver at Rs. 4,000 per month for up to 300 students, Gold at Rs. 7,000 per month for up to 800 students, and Diamond at Rs. 12,000 per month for up to 1,200 students. Paying yearly reduces the equivalent monthly rate by 10% — Rs. 43,200, Rs. 75,600 and Rs. 129,600 per year respectively. These are the published prices; there is no need to request a quote to find out what EduPortal costs.
Choose by enrollment. Silver suits institutes up to 300 students that need the core school management modules. Gold covers up to 800 students and adds the parent and teacher mobile apps, academic planning and POS, which is why it is the most common choice for growing schools. Diamond covers up to 1,200 students and adds the finance, hostel and premium customization modules. The comparison table above lists exactly which modules are in each plan.
Every module marked for your plan in the comparison table above is included in the monthly price — there is no separate per-module licence fee. Your plan covers your student roll up to its limit — 300 on Silver, 800 on Gold, 1,200 on Diamond — and those modules stay available for as long as your subscription is active.
Four things are billed separately from the plan price. SMS credits are not included — text messages are charged against your own SMS gateway account, so you pay your provider for the volume you actually send. WhatsApp messaging through the WhatsApp Business API is charged per conversation by Meta, again on your own account. One-time onboarding work such as initial setup and importing your existing student, staff and fee records is quoted separately, because it depends on how much data you are bringing across and what condition it is in. On-site staff training is also quoted separately, since it depends on how many campuses and staff need to be covered. Everything else you see in the comparison table is in the monthly price.
Yes. Yearly billing is 10% less than paying month to month.
